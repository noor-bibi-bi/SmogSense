## ⏱️ Video Timeline & Script

| Timestamp | Visual on Screen | Spoken Script |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:15** | Title slide / Lahore smog photo / Open SmogSense app | *"In Lahore, winter air pollution reaches hazardous levels. Every morning at 6 AM, school principals and parents face a critical question: Is it safe for children to have outdoor recess and PE today? Existing apps only show what happened yesterday. That's why we built SmogSense."* |
| **0:15 – 0:35** | SmogSense live app — Show **Proceed Outdoors** vs **Move Indoors** hero card | *"SmogSense is an AI-powered City Intelligence platform. At its core is an unequivocal directive: **Proceed Outdoors** or **Move Indoors**. Our trained XGBoost model evaluates atmospheric lag features and weather forecasts to predict tomorrow's PM2.5 levels against a strict 100 µg/m³ safety threshold."* |
| **0:35 – 0:50** | Scroll down to the **7-Day Trend Chart** & Supporting KPIs | *"Here, administrators can see the 7-day PM2.5 trajectory leading seamlessly into tomorrow's predicted point, along with real-time sensor readings, delta changes, and Punjab EPA safety boundaries."* |
| **0:50 – 1:10** | Click **"School Alerts"** modal & click **"Send Test Alert"** / show toast & browser notification | *"Crucially, SmogSense is an active early warning system. School administrators can register for automated 6:00 AM dispatches. When we trigger or simulate a smog surge, instant emergency push notifications are dispatched to all registered schools before buses leave."* |
| **1:10 – 1:25** | Toggle Demo Scenarios (Proceed & Move Indoors states) & Show Code / Docs | *"Backed by a robust FastAPI microservice and validated on multi-year Lahore atmospheric telemetry, SmogSense brings actionable intelligence to protecting children's health. Thank you!"* |

## 🎥 Recording Checklist
- [ ] Screen resolution set to 1080p (1920x1080).
- [ ] Browser zoomed to ~100% or 110% for clear text readability.
- [ ] Microphone tested for clear audio.
- [ ] Browser notifications enabled to demonstrate the live push notification.
- [ ] Record one clean run-through (60–90 seconds) and save a backup copy.
